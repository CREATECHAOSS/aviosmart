Our process

From operational pressure to controlled execution.

Every project begins with your specific operational challenge. We define the support required, establish a clear scope, and build an execution model around the products, suppliers, regulations and contractual conditions involved.

  1. PRE-FLIGHT

    We understand the challenge

    You share your purchasing backlog, project requirements, negotiation needs, internal constraints and expected outcomes. We identify where execution is being delayed.

  2. TAKEOFF

    We assess the landscape

    We review the products or services, supplier landscape, aviation standards, technical requirements and contracts — and decide whether you need a complete process or a specific sub-process.

  3. CRUISE

    We propose the operating model

    Scope, responsibilities, team structure and method of execution. Once approved, we present the commercial terms and formalize the service agreement.

  4. LANDING

    We execute with visibility

    Experienced buyers execute under senior guidance, with visibility over requirements, supplier commitments, contract milestones, delivery risks and progress.
